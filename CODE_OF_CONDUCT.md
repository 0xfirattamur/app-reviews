# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while addressing
the actual technical impact of a contribution. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruptive
behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- explaining disagreements with evidence
- making room for different levels of experience
- accepting correction and repairing harm
- keeping bug reports free of private credentials and reviewer data

## Scope

This standard applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, and public interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities belong in
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/0xfirattamur/app-reviews/security/advisories/new),
not a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed in context. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close threads, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to
protect the community. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
